# Tableau Visualization Plan

The assignment requires at least 12 different visualization approaches. This plan gives each chart a distinct analytical purpose.

## 01 — Multi-Line GDP Trend
**Goal:** Compare Real GDP trajectories from 2016–2025.  
**Dimensions:** Year, State  
**Measure:** Real GDP  
**Key analysis:** Long-run economic size and major disruptions.

## 02 — Regional Small Multiples
**Goal:** Compare state trajectories within Census regions.  
**Dimensions:** Region, State, Year  
**Measure:** Real GDP

## 03 — 2025 Horizontal Ranking Bar Chart
**Goal:** Rank selected states by latest-year Real GDP.  
**Filter:** Year = 2025  
**Dimension:** State  
**Measure:** Real GDP

## 04 — 2016 vs. 2025 Slope Chart
**Goal:** Compare start/end values and direction of change.  
**Years:** 2016 and 2025 only

## 05 — Filled U.S. Map
**Goal:** Show geographic distribution of GDP.  
**Geographic field:** State  
**Measure:** Real GDP  
**Filter:** Latest year or interactive year selector

## 06 — State × Year Heat Map
**Goal:** Reveal strong/weak state-year patterns.  
**Rows:** State  
**Columns:** Year  
**Color:** YoY growth or indexed GDP

## 07 — 10-Year Growth Bar Chart
**Goal:** Rank states by percentage growth from 2016–2025.  
**Measure:** Ten-Year Growth %

## 08 — Starting Size vs. Growth Scatter Plot
**Goal:** Explore whether starting GDP is associated with 10-year percentage growth.  
**X:** 2016 Real GDP  
**Y:** Ten-Year Growth %  
**Detail:** State  
**Optional:** Trend line

## 09 — Regional Box Plot
**Goal:** Compare distributions across regions.  
**Dimension:** Region  
**Measure:** Real GDP or annual growth

## 10 — Annual Growth Histogram
**Goal:** Show the distribution of yearly state GDP growth rates.  
**Measure:** YoY Growth %  
**Bins:** Choose readable intervals

## 11 — 2025 Treemap
**Goal:** Show the concentration of combined GDP among selected states.  
**Size:** Real GDP  
**Label:** State  
**Filter:** Year = 2025

## 12 — Rank / Bump Chart
**Goal:** Show changes in relative state rank across the decade.  
**X:** Year  
**Y:** State Rank  
**Detail:** State

## Written Analysis Pattern

For every major chart:

1. **Observation** — What pattern is visible?
2. **Evidence** — Which states/years/values support it?
3. **Interpretation** — What does the pattern suggest?
4. **Significance** — Why does it matter to the research question?
