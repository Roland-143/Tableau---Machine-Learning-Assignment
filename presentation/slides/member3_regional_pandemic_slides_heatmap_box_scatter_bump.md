# Member 3 Slides (≈2–3 minutes)

## Slide 1 — Regional GDP Growth Patterns (Heat Map)
- 2020 is the only broad contraction: 15 of 16 states fell (Arizona +1.81%).
- 2021 rebound: all states grew, 3.57% (Pennsylvania) to 9.37% (Florida).
- 2022–2025 average growth: Texas 5.05%, Florida 4.12%; Pennsylvania 1.57%, Ohio 1.64%.
*Notes (~45s):* "This heat map shows year-over-year Real GDP growth by state, grouped by region. The dark 2020 column and bright 2021 column stand out. Afterwards, Southern states like Texas and Florida kept growing faster. Regions are only our selected four states each."

## Slide 2 — Pandemic Impact and Recovery (Box Plot + Scatter Plot)
- 2020 median growth: Midwest −3.11%, Northeast −2.91%, South −1.21%, West −0.74%.
- Illinois −4.96% (largest decline); Arizona +1.81% (outlier); West widest spread.
- 2021: Florida +9.37% strongest; Pennsylvania +3.57% weakest.
- 2020 vs 2021 growth correlation +0.57: bigger declines were not followed by bigger rebounds (descriptive, not tested).
*Notes (~60s):* "The box plot compares 2020 across regions. The scatter plots each state's 2020 growth against 2021 growth. States that fell least tended to rebound more, so recovery was not just catching up. We don't claim causation."

## Slide 3 — Changes in State GDP Rankings (Bump Chart)
- Top 8 ranks unchanged in 2019, 2020, 2021, 2025.
- Arizona 16→15 and Indiana 15→16 in 2020, persisting to 2025; Washington 10→9 and New Jersey 9→10 by 2025.
- Conclusion: the pandemic changed growth rates but barely changed rankings.
*Notes (~45s):* "The bump chart ranks states by Real GDP. Rankings were remarkably stable; only two swaps occurred."
