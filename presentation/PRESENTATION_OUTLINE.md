# Presentation Outline

Recommended length: approximately 9–10 slides, adjusted to the course presentation time.

1. **Title**
2. **Research Question & Project Objective**
3. **Dataset, State Selection & Methodology**
4. **10-Year GDP Trends**
5. **2025 Economic Size / Geographic Comparison**
6. **Long-Term Growth Comparison**
7. **Regional Patterns**
8. **2020 Disruption & Recovery**
9. **Rank Changes / Outliers / Additional Findings**
10. **Key Findings, Limitations & Conclusion**

## Presentation Principle

The assignment requires 12 visualization approaches, but the presentation does not need to give equal speaking time to every chart. Highlight the strongest visuals and use the report/Tableau workbook to demonstrate full coverage.

## Team Requirement

Every contributor must appear in the recorded presentation and should have a meaningful speaking role.
