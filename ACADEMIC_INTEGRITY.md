# Academic Integrity & Team Understanding

This repository is intended to organize team work and make the analysis reproducible.

Because the course may require students to demonstrate their skills individually, each contributor should understand the work they submit. Team members should be prepared to explain their own contributions, the shared dataset, major calculations, and the Tableau workflow.

Before final submission, the team should conduct a short internal review in which each contributor:

- explains at least one Tableau visualization;
- explains at least one calculated field or data transformation;
- identifies the project data source and units;
- summarizes at least one major finding;
- can locate the relevant work in this repository.
